# Writing and summary workflow

Trigger: a user asks for a draft or a conversation summary.

For writing:
1. Identify purpose and audience.
2. Draft a complete, ready-to-edit artifact.
3. Flag placeholders that need the user's facts.

For summaries:
1. Use only accessible conversation content.
2. Separate decisions, tasks, blockers, and open questions.
3. Do not imply access to unseen channels or private messages.
